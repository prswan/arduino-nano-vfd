| STROBE |  9 | 10 | CLK    |

PL2 SPI & Power Connector
-------------------------

---------------------------------
|        |  1 |  2 | Fil1       |
| Vbb+   |  3 |  4 | BLANK      |
| GND    |  5 |  6 | GND        |
| Fil2   |  7 |  8 | SDOUT/SDIN | TP patched
| STROBE |  9 | 10 | CLK        |
| Vcc    | 11 | 12 | SDIN/SDOUT | TP patched
---------------------------------

Vbb+ = +36V Anode
Fil  = AC filament Vbb offset supply

Pin 1 reserved for Vbb- (-24V) on negative voltage driver boards.
